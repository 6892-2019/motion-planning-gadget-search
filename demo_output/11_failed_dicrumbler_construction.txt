Claim (FALSE): the series-toggle network simulates a dicrumbler.

Construction: 2 instance(s), 1 wire(s), external = ['L', 'R']
Induced gadget: 2 reachable config(s), 2 traversal mode(s).
  induced reversible? True   target reversible? False
  induced DAG?        False   target DAG?        True
Simulates target: False  (induced is NOT equivalent to target)

Verified: False.  The induced gadget is a toggle (reversible); the
dicrumbler is irreversible, so no wiring of toggles can produce it.
This agrees with refute_simulation in Examples 3–4.
